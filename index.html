Lorenz Curve & Gini Coefficient ?

📖 How to Read This Chart (Simple Version)

  • The diagonal dashed line (grey): This is the "Line of Equality" — it means everyone earns exactly the same amount. If your hotel followed this line, a housekeeper and a GM would earn the same!
  • The solid blue curve: This is your hotel's actual salary distribution. It shows how much of the total salary pie is earned by what percentage of people.
  • The gap between the two lines: The bigger the gap, the more unequal your salary distribution. A small gap = relatively fair; a big gap = top earners take a large share.
  • Gini Coefficient number:
    • 🟢 Below 0.35: Relatively equal — good balance between levels
    • 🟡 0.35 - 0.45: Moderate inequality — typical for most hotels
    • 🔴 Above 0.45: High inequality — senior levels may be overpaid relative to frontline

💡 Example: If the curve shows that the bottom 50% of employees earn only 20% of total salary, while the top 10% earn 40%, that's a significant gap.

📖 How to Read What-If Analysis

  • Total Salary Budget Increase (%): Drag the slider to simulate different budget increase scenarios (e.g., 3% = hotel decides to increase total salary pool by 3%)
  • Low CR Weight: Higher value means more budget is allocated to employees with Compa-Ratio < 0.8 (below market). 2.0x means they get double the average allocation
  • High Performer Weight: Higher value means top performers get more than average share of the budget
  • Allocation Matrix: Shows how the budget is distributed across categories based on the weights you set
  • Projected New CR: Estimates what the average Compa-Ratio would be after applying this budget allocation
